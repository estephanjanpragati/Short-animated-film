## Reel A: Abraham on the mountain
**Series:** The Bible, Start to Finish / बाइबल: शुरू से अंत तक · Genesis 22
**Length:** about 70 s

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Abraham was 75 when he left with God's promise; 100 when Isaac was born | Genesis 12:4, 21:5 |
| "Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love" … Moriah | Genesis 22:2 |
| Rose early in the morning | Genesis 22:3 |
| "We will worship and come back to you" | Genesis 22:5 |
| "Where is the lamb?" / "God will provide the lamb" | Genesis 22:7–8 |
| Angel: "Now I know that you fear God"; ram in the thicket; "The LORD will provide" | Genesis 22:11–14 |
| Abraham reasoned God could raise Isaac from the dead | Hebrews 11:19 |
| Moriah is where Jerusalem later stood; God gave his own Son | 2 Chronicles 3:1; John 3:16; Romans 8:32 |

### English
1. HOOK: God asked an old man to give up the one thing he had waited twenty-five years for. What would you do?
2. Abraham was seventy-five when God promised him a son. He waited. And waited. At a hundred, Isaac was finally born.
3. Then one night God said: take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love, and offer him on Mount Moriah.
4. Early the next morning, Abraham got up and went. No arguing. No delay.
5. Three days of walking. At the mountain he tells his servants: we will worship, and we will come back to you. We. Both of us.
6. Isaac carries the wood and asks, Father, where's the lamb? Abraham says, God will provide the lamb, my son.
7. He builds the altar. He lifts the knife. And an angel calls: Stop! Now I know you fear God.
8. A ram is caught in the bushes. Abraham names the place: The Lord Will Provide.
9. LESSON: The Bible says Abraham believed God could even raise Isaac from the dead. That's faith: obeying before you understand, because you trust the one who is asking.
10. Centuries later, on a hill near that same place, God did what he never let Abraham do. He gave his own Son.
11. CTA: What is God asking you to trust him with today? Tell me in the comments.

## Reel B: Aaron and the golden calf
**Series:** The Bible, Start to Finish / बाइबल: शुरू से अंत तक · Exodus 32
**Length:** about 70 s

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Moses on the mountain forty days | Exodus 24:18 |
| "As for this Moses… we don't know what has become of him. Make us gods" | Exodus 32:1 |
| Aaron took the gold earrings, shaped it with a tool, made a calf, built an altar, "Tomorrow is a feast to the LORD" | Exodus 32:2–5 |
| The people ate, drank and rose up to play | Exodus 32:6 |
| Moses broke the tablets | Exodus 32:19 |
| "What did this people do to you, that you brought so great a sin on them?" | Exodus 32:21 |
| "You know the people, that they are set on evil… I threw it into the fire, and out came this calf" | Exodus 32:22–24 |
| Adam: "The woman you gave me…" | Genesis 3:12 |
| Moses prayed for Aaron | Deuteronomy 9:20 |
| Aaron still served as high priest | Leviticus 8 |
| Whoever conceals sins won't prosper; whoever confesses and forsakes finds mercy | Proverbs 28:13 |

### English
1. HOOK: The worst excuse in the Bible? I threw the gold in the fire… and out came this calf!
2. Moses is up on Mount Sinai for forty days. Down below, the people get impatient: we don't know what happened to this Moses. Make us gods!
3. And Aaron, Moses' own brother, doesn't say no. He collects their gold earrings, shapes it with a tool, and makes a calf. Then he builds an altar and says: tomorrow is a feast to the Lord.
4. The people eat, drink, and party.
5. Moses comes down. He sees the calf and the dancing. The tablets shatter.
6. He asks Aaron: what did these people do to you, that you led them into such a sin?
7. And Aaron says: don't be angry. You know these people, they're set on evil. They gave me gold, I threw it in the fire, and out came this calf!
8. LESSON: See what he did? He blamed the people. He blamed the fire. Everyone except himself. It's the oldest trick in the book, ever since Adam said: the woman you gave me.
9. But here's the good news. Moses prayed for Aaron, and God still let him serve as high priest. Whoever hides their sins won't prosper, but whoever confesses and leaves them finds mercy.
10. CTA: When did you last say, it wasn't my fault? Be honest in the comments.
