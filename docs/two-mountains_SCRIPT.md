# Reel: Two Mountains: Abraham and Aaron
**Series:** Have You Ever Thought? / कभी सोचा है? · **Day 2**
**Length:** about 65–70 s · vertical 1440×2560 · English + Hindi, same picture

Note on names: the man who "played innocent" when Moses came down was **Aaron** (हारून), Moses' brother, who made the golden calf. Joshua was up the mountain with Moses and had no part in it (Exodus 32:17).
